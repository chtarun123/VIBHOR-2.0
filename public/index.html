About Us

Why VIBHOR Exists

VIBHOR (meaning “splendour” in Sanskrit) is a single, organised journey through 120 heritage stories — ten per category, in every region. Instead of making you search, we lay out the entire country as four regions × twelve categories × every state — so you can simply pick a door and walk in.

Every story is a real place or tradition with its own history, features and cultural importance, linked to its Wikipedia reference — and every page streams related videos straight from YouTube, so the heritage doesn't just sit on a page: it moves, it dances, it cooks, it sings.

This is a fan-made, educational curation. Images are stock and illustrative placeholders.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

VIBHOR is an immersive Indian-heritage web app: a curated library (four regions × twelve categories, 120 real stories, each with history, features, importance and a Wikipedia reference) plus a real community layer — accounts, challenges, a leaderboard and a shared Heritage Archive, all served by its own Node + SQLite backend from one command.

The four cultural regions — North (UP, Rajasthan, Punjab, Himachal, Uttarakhand, Delhi, MP, J&K), South (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana), East (West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar, Jharkhand, Assam, Chhattisgarh) and West (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Goa). The interactive map covers all 36 states and UTs, each coloured by its cultural region.

Yes — guests can browse, search, use the map, listen to stories, chat with the AI Guide and play the quiz with no sign-in. A free account only unlocks saved progress, challenge and archive submissions, saved journeys and the leaderboard. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed; nothing is stored in plain text.

Everything is local-first: the SQLite database file lives inside the project folder, uploads land in server/uploads, and guest progress stays in your browser until you sign in and it merges into your account. Your first-party data never leaves your machine.
